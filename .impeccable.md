## Design Context

### Users
Visitors discovering fynxlab's Windows products and potential clients assessing the studio for software development work. They need to understand the products, evaluate the studio's approach, and find a direct contact route.

### Brand Personality
Professional, considered, dependable. Confident without grandiose claims; concise, human product writing.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark industrial design archive: deep navy-blue backgrounds, subtle drafting grids, framed product specimens, precise annotations, and strong sans-serif typography. The user's phoenix logo is the visual anchor; its amber, orange, and magenta are reserved for sparse, high-contrast accents rather than full-page fills. No neon glows, generic tech illustrations, or gratuitous motion. English is the default language; Chinese is equally accessible.

### Design Principles
- Let the original logo, real products, and clear writing carry the story.
- Keep the route from discovery to product or contact obvious.
- Treat English and Chinese as first-class, individually indexable pages.
- Prefer restraint, legibility, accessibility, and fast static delivery over spectacle.
